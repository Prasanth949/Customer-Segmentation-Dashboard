# 📊 Customer Segmentation Dashboard | Power BI & SQL
---

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using **Power BI, SQL, Excel, Power Query, and DAX**.

The objective is to identify valuable customer segments, analyze purchasing patterns, evaluate product category performance, understand payment preferences, and generate actionable business insights.

This project demonstrates the use of data analysis and visualization techniques to support data-driven business decision-making.

---

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Understand customer demographics
- Analyze revenue and order performance
- Identify high-performing product categories
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Compare shopping mall performance
- Analyze monthly revenue trends
- Identify valuable customer segments
- Generate actionable business insights

---

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **SQL** – Data analysis and querying
- **Excel** – Data preparation and analysis

📈 Key Insights

### 👥 Customer Demographics

- Female customers are the dominant customer segment.
- Female customers contribute the highest share of revenue and purchases.
- Customers aged **26–65** account for the majority of shopping activity.
- Customers under 18 and 65+ contribute comparatively lower purchase volumes.

### 🛍️ Product Category Performance

- **Clothing** is the highest revenue-generating category.
- **Shoes** and **Technology** are also major contributors to overall revenue.
- **Books** and **Souvenirs** generate comparatively lower revenue.

### 💳 Payment Method Analysis

- **Cash** is the most preferred payment method.
- **Credit Card** is the second-highest contributor.
- **Debit Card** has the lowest usage.
- Digital payment adoption can potentially be increased through discounts, cashback offers, and loyalty rewards.

### 🏬 Shopping Mall Performance

- **Mall of Istanbul** generates the highest revenue among the analyzed shopping malls.
- **Emaar Square Mall** and **Forum Istanbul** contribute comparatively lower revenue.
- High-performing locations can be prioritized for marketing and inventory planning.

### 📅 Monthly Revenue Trend

- Revenue reaches its highest level in **January**.
- Revenue declines during the first half of the year and remains relatively stable afterward with minor fluctuations.
- Lower-performing months provide opportunities for targeted promotional campaigns.

---

💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus marketing campaigns on the high-value **26–65 age segment**.
- Continue strong inventory and promotional efforts for **Clothing, Shoes, and Technology**.
- Introduce targeted promotions for lower-performing categories such as **Books and Souvenirs**.
- Encourage digital payment adoption through cashback offers, discounts, and loyalty rewards.
- Prioritize high-performing shopping malls for inventory expansion and marketing campaigns.
- Analyze seasonal sales patterns and introduce promotions during lower-performing months.
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns based on customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
