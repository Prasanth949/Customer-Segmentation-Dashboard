Customer Segmentation Dashboard

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior using Power BI, SQL, Excel, Power Query, and DAX.

The objective is to identify valuable customer segments, analyze purchasing patterns, evaluate product category performance, and generate actionable business insights.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* Power BI
* SQL
* Excel
* Power Query
* DAX

🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior
* Understand customer demographics
* Analyze revenue and order performance
* Identify high-performing product categories
* Analyze payment method preferences
* Compare shopping mall performance
* Analyze monthly revenue trends
* Identify valuable customer segments
* Generate actionable business insights

📈 Key Insights

* Female customers are the dominant customer segment.
* Customers aged 26–65 account for the majority of shopping activity.
* Clothing is the highest revenue-generating product category.
* Shoes and Technology are also major revenue contributors.
* Cash is the most preferred payment method.
* Mall of Istanbul generates the highest revenue among the analyzed shopping malls.
* January records the highest monthly revenue.
* Books and Souvenirs generate comparatively lower revenue.

💡 Business Recommendations

* Focus marketing campaigns on the high-value 26–65 customer segment.
* Continue strong inventory and promotional efforts for Clothing, Shoes, and Technology.
* Use targeted promotions to improve performance of lower-revenue categories.
* Encourage digital payment adoption through cashback, discounts, and loyalty rewards.
* Prioritize high-performing shopping locations for inventory and marketing activities.
* Use seasonal promotions during lower-performing months.
